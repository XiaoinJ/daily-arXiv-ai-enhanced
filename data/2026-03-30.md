### [52] [On nonconvex constellations among primes I](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25896)
*Fred B. Holt*

Main category: math.NT

TL;DR: 该论文将k元组猜想的方法应用于Engelsma反例（长度为J=459、跨度为|s|=3242的窄星座），追踪了58个反例从不可容许驱动项到首次出现的演化过程，通过广度优先搜索计算发现这些反例在9.7×10⁷³之前不会出现。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 研究Engelsma反例（长度为459、跨度为3242的窄星座）在素数分布中的行为，验证这些反例是否会在特定范围内出现，从而深化对k元组猜想和素数星座分布的理解。

Method: 将k元组猜想的方法应用于Engelsma反例，从不可容许驱动项开始追踪反例在间隙循环G(p#)中的演化，使用primordial坐标表示每个可容许实例，通过广度优先穷举搜索到G(211#)，然后开发深度优先搜索策略寻找能通过埃拉托色尼筛法的实例。

Result: 计算表明所有58个(459,3242)-反例在9.7×10⁷³之前都不会出现。对每个反例计算了其在长度为J=459的星座中的渐近相对比例，并分析了这些反例的工作原理。

Conclusion: Engelsma反例在极大范围内不会出现，这为素数星座分布提供了重要见解。需要进一步开发深度优先搜索策略来寻找能通过埃拉托色尼筛法的实例，以完全验证这些反例的行为。

Abstract: Extending our work on the $k$-tuple conjecture, we apply those methods to the Engelsma counterexamples (narrow constellations) of length $J=459$ and span $|s|=3242$. We track the evolution of these $58$ counterexamples from inadmissible driving terms starting in the cycle of gaps ${\mathcal G}(11^\#)$ up through their first appearance in ${\mathcal G}(113^\#)$. We continue developing primorial coordinates for each admissible instance through a breadth-first exhaustive search through ${\mathcal G}(211^\#)$, at which point we need to develop strategies for depth-first searches for an instance that would survive Eratosthenes sieve. Our calculations show that {\em none} of the $(459,3242)$-counterexamples occur before $9.7\,E73$.
  For each of the $58$ Engelsma $(459,3242)$-counterexamples we calculate its asymptotic relative population, among other constellations of length $J=459$, and we study how these counterexamples work.

### [53] [On a Möbius double sum](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.25961)
*Olivier Ramaré,Sebastian Zuniga-Alterman*

Main category: math.NT

TL;DR: 研究双和$S_\varepsilon(X)=\sum_{d,e\le X}\frac{μ(d)μ(e)}{[d,e]^{1+\varepsilon}}$的均匀上界估计，特别关注$\varepsilon$接近$0^+$的情况


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 这类表达式在解析数论中自然出现，特别是在某些平方均值中的对角贡献，在黎曼ζ函数及相关L函数的零密度估计中起重要作用

Method: 研究双和$S_\varepsilon(X)$的收敛性和上界估计，分析其在$\varepsilon=0$时的收敛性，并建立不同$X$范围内的均匀上界

Result: 建立了$S_\varepsilon(X)$在各种$X$范围内的均匀上界，特别关注$\varepsilon$接近$0^+$的情况

Conclusion: 该研究为解析数论中重要的双和表达式提供了关键的均匀上界估计，对零密度估计等应用具有重要意义

Abstract: We study the double sum $S_\varepsilon(X)$$=$$\sum_{\substack{d,e\le
  X}}\frac{μ(d)μ(e)}{[d,e]^{1+\varepsilon}}$, which converges even in the case $\varepsilon=0$, where $μ$ denotes the Möbius function and $[d,e]$ is the least common multiple of $d$ and $e$.
  Such expressions arise naturally in analytic number theory, notably as the diagonal contribution in certain squared mean values, and they play a significant role in zero-density estimates for the Riemann zeta function and related $L$-functions. We establish uniform upper bounds for $S_\varepsilon(X)$ across various ranges of $X$, with particular emphasis on the case $\varepsilon$ close to $0^+$.

</details>
