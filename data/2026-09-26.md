### [1] [Representation World Model: Learning States, Transition and Executable Plans in Representation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29171)
*Yijun Yuan,Weicheng Zheng,Weibang Wang,Minghui Qin,Chang Sun,Junhao Huang,Kenan Li,Anmin Liu,Yicheng Yao,Hang Zhao*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: RWM 将规划直接嵌入表示几何，在潜在空间中构造当前状态与目标状态之间的路径，用逆动力学恢复动作，无需递归推演或动作空间搜索。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有世界模型通常把潜在表示与显式动力学模型联合学习，并通过搜索、优化或基于策略的预测来完成规划，计算开销大且依赖递归推演。作者希望将规划直接融入表示空间结构，避免传统世界模型规划的复杂流程。

Method: RWM 直接学习状态、转移和可执行计划。它通过逆动力学监督，在由端点表示构造的潜在路径上局部施加约束，要求这些路径保留任务相关的状态与转移信息，从而塑造表示几何。推理阶段直接在当前表示与目标表示之间构造潜在路径，并用逆动力学恢复对应动作，不进行递归推演或动作空间搜索。

Result: 在连续控制基准上验证了 RWM 用于直接规划的有效性；在机器人操作任务上进一步显示其可扩展到更复杂的具身控制任务。

Conclusion: 结果表明，直接在表示空间中进行规划，为传统世界模型规划提供了一种有前景的替代方案。

Abstract: We propose the Representation World Model (RWM), which learns states, transitions, and executable plans directly in representation space. Unlike existing world models that typically learn latent representations together with explicit dynamics models and perform planning through search, optimization, or policy-based prediction, RWM directly incorporates planning into the learned representation geometry. RWM learns the representation geometry by applying inverse-dynamics supervision locally along latent paths constructed from endpoint representations, requiring these paths to preserve task-relevant state and transition information. At inference, planning is performed by directly constructing a latent path between the current and goal representations, with inverse dynamics used to recover the corresponding actions, without recursive rollouts or action-space search. Experiments on continuous-control benchmarks demonstrate the effectiveness of RWM for direct planning, while results on robotic manipulation further show its potential to extend to more complex embodied control tasks. These results suggest that planning directly in representation space provides a promising alternative to conventional world-model planning.

### [2] [RAPID: Robot Agentic Programming from Demonstrations](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.30249)
*Yuyao Liu,Jiayuan Mao,David Hsu,Leslie Pack Kaelbling,Tomás Lozano-Pérez*

Main category: cs.RO

TL;DR: RAPID: 从单次人类视觉演示自动生成、验证并迭代优化机器人程序，实现跨位姿/形状/材质/环境的泛化操作。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 编码智能体在复杂编程任务中表现优异，但将其潜力迁移到机器人系统需要一个可被代码智能体迭代优化的工作流。传统模仿学习/演示方法依赖大量数据或难以跨场景复用，因此本文希望仅凭单次视觉人类演示，自动构建可测试、可执行、可验证的机器人程序，并具备泛化能力。

Method: 提出 RAPID（Robot Agentic Programming from Demonstrations）框架，基于单次视觉演示自动推断三项要素：(i) 可测试的任务规范，(ii) 用于机器人执行的动作原语，(iii) 用于程序执行与验证的交互环境，并在此闭环中进行代码生成、验证与迭代精化。为提升跨演示场景的可复用性，采用以物体为中心的关系式程序表示：将动作原语表达为轨迹优化程序以实现物体层面的运动效果，并通过关系约束在运行时绑定场景特定几何。

Result: 在仿真中于八个高难度、富接触的非抓取（nonprehensile）操作任务以及 LIBERO-Pro 基准的常规抓取操作任务上进行评测；并在真实 Franka 机械臂上对全部八个非抓取任务完成部署。所有实验中 RAPID 均表现优异，在物体位姿、形状、材质与环境四个方面均展现出泛化能力。

Conclusion: RAPID 表明，编码智能体的"生成-验证-精化"闭环可有效用于机器人编程：仅凭单次视觉演示即可自动获得可测试任务规范、动作原语与验证环境，并通过物体为中心的关系式表示实现跨场景复用。仿真与真机结果验证了其在富接触非抓取及标准抓取操作上的强性能与多维度泛化能力。

Abstract: Coding agents have demonstrated enormous success in solving complex programming problems. To leverage their potential for robot systems, this work introduces Robot Agentic Programming from Demonstrations (RAPID), which automatically generates, verifies, and refines robot programs, given a single visual human demonstration. The iterative agentic loop of code refinement requires several key ingredients: (i) a testable task specification, (ii) action primitives for robot execution, and (iii) an interactive environment for program execution and verification. RAPID infers all three from the demonstration automatically. To make the resulting program reusable beyond the demonstration setting, RAPID uses an object-centric relational program representation that focuses on the underlying structure of the demonstrated strategy rather than the specific motion per se: it expresses the action primitives as trajectory-optimization programs that realize object-level motion effects, while composing them through relational constraints that capture scene-specific geometry at run time. We evaluated RAPID in simulation on eight challenging contact-rich nonprehensile manipulation tasks as well as general prehensile manipulation tasks in the LIBERO-Pro benchmark. We also successfully deployed it on a real Franka arm and evaluated on all eight nonprehensile tasks. In all experiments, RAPID demonstrated strong performance, with generalization over object pose, shape, material, and environment. Website: https://yuyaoliu.me/projects/rapid.
